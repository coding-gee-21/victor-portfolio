Industrial Attachment & Work Experience

Electrical & Electronics Engineering Intern

Spenza Limited — Kerugoya, Kenya

May 2026 – July 2026
Executive Abstract: Completed an intensive 12-week industrial engineering attachment at Spenza Limited, a 150-tonne/day continuous grain processing plant. Hands-on duties encompassed low-resistance grounding infrastructure design, 3-phase motor protection diagnostics, control panel rewiring, and preventive electromechanical servicing in compliance with local electrical safety regulations and ISO 45000:2018 standard operating procedures.

1. Grounding & Earthing Systems Design

  • Earth Pit Construction: Excavated 2.5m earth pits and fabricated 1m x 1m square copper earth meshes using precision riveting equipment.
  • Biochemical Conditioning: Applied layered sodium chloride (salt) and compacted wood charcoal surrounding earthing meshes to maintain low soil resistivity.
  • Equipment Bonding: Terminated heavy-duty galvanized aluminum flat conductors using wall drills to bond 10+ three-phase induction motors, pumps, packaging units, and compressors to ground.

2. Control Room & Electrical Wiring

  • Conduit & Tray Installation: Executed masonry wall chiseling, PVC conduit routing, and multi-core power cable laying across overhead metal cable trays to supply new control rooms.
  • Power & Lighting Distribution: Wired and terminated 13A socket outlets, two-way lighting switches, ceiling roses, consumer units, and 225W exterior security floodlights.

3. Motor Maintenance & Control Diagnostics

  • Motor Rating Mismatch Correction: Diagnosed severe thermal overload on 250 kW roller mill induction motors caused by installation mismatches; dismounted, interchanged, and realigned drive shafts.
  • Interlocked Logic Circuits: Designed and installed dual-contactor forward-reverse control circuits with electrical interlocking for maintenance metal bending machinery.
  • Protection Relays: Replaced degraded phase contactors and calibrated Direct-On-Line (DOL) thermal overload relays (OLR) to eliminate single-phasing conditions.

4. Power Systems & Electromechanical Diagnostics

  • Conveyance Maintenance: Conducted belt tensioning, drive belt replacements, gearbox oil inspections, and bearing greasing on bucket elevators, screw conveyors, and extruders.
  • Short-Circuit Analysis: Modeled short-circuit fault capacity ($I_{sc} \approx 27.5\text{ kA}$) for the facility's 1000 kVA, 33kV/420V Dyn11 YOCEAN transformer to verify switchgear breaking thresholds.
