Industrial Engineering Attachment

Spenza Limited — 150 TPD Grain Processing Plant | Technical Log & Field Photo Records

Copper Earth Mesh Fabrication & Riveting

1. Industrial Grounding & Earthing Mesh Construction

  • Earth Pit Excavation: Participated in digging 2.5m earth pits and fabricated 1m × 1m square copper earth meshes using heavy-duty riveting tools.
  • Soil Conditioning: Applied structured sodium chloride (salt) and compacted wood charcoal layers to lower earth pit impedance ($R_{earth} < 5\,\Omega$).
  • Equipotential Bonding: Routed and anchored galvanized aluminum conductors across plant masonry walls to bond 10+ 3-phase induction motors, compressors, and packaging machines to ground.
Control Panel Wiring & Switchgear Setup

2. Control Room Wiring & Interlocked Circuits

  • Logic Interlocking: Assembled dual-contactor forward-reverse starter circuits with electrical contact interlocking for sheet-metal bending machinery.
  • Component Installation: Terminated Miniature Circuit Breakers (MCBs), Molded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCBs), thermal overload relays, and phase failure protection modules.
  • Sub-Distribution Setup: Wired 13A industrial sockets, ceiling switches, and 225W exterior LED floodlights for new plant expansion wings.
250 kW Motor Realignment & Diagnostic Check

3. 3-Phase Motor Maintenance & Protection Calibration

  • Rating Correction: Diagnosed thermal tripping on 250 kW roller mill induction motors caused by mismatched drive ratings; unmounted, swapped, and re-aligned motor shafts.
  • Overload Calibration: Calibrated Direct-On-Line (DOL) thermal overload relays ($I_{FLC} \times 1.10$) to prevent phase failure damage and continuous winding overheating.
  • Mechanical Overhaul: Conducted bearing greasing, drive belt tensioning, and gearbox oil level checks across bucket elevators and screw conveyors.
PVC Conduit Routing & Cable Containment

4. Cable Containment & Overhead Conduit Piping

  • Wall Chiseling & Piping: Executed precise masonry wall chasing and PVC conduit fitting for hidden multi-core supply lines.
  • Overhead Cable Trays: Installed metal cable tray suspension networks and pulled heavy armored multi-core cables across continuous processing halls.
  • Short-Circuit Analysis: Evaluated local fault capacity ($I_{sc} \approx 27.5\text{ kA}$) for the facility's 1000 kVA 33kV/420V Dyn11 distribution transformer to verify circuit breaker trip ratings.
